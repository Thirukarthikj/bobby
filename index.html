What We Offers

We are specialized in variety of Packing Bags & Speciality films

Ld Bags

LD bags, made from Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE), are flexible, durable, and water-resistant, perfect for packaging food and textiles. They are recyclable, with efforts underway to improve their eco-friendliness.

HM Bags

HM bags, made from High Molecular High-Density Polyethylene (HM-HDPE), are strong, durable, and resistant to punctures and tears. They are commonly used for heavy-duty packaging and can be recycled.

LD Roll

LD rolls refer to rolls of Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE) film, known for their flexibility, clarity, and moisture resistance. They are used for various applications such as packaging, wrapping, and protecting goods during transit.

PP Bags

PP bags, made from polypropylene, are durable and moisture-resistant, ideal for packaging food, textiles, and various products. They are recyclable and come in woven and non-woven varieties for different packaging needs.

PET Pouches

PET pouches are clear, strong, and provide moisture and oxygen barrier properties, making them ideal for packaging. They preserve product freshness and ensure high visibility of contents.

BOPP Bags

BOPP bags, made from Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene, are durable, transparent, and resistant to moisture and oils, commonly used for packaging snacks, textiles, and various retail products.

PVC Pouches

PVC bags, made from Polyvinyl Chloride, are durable and moisture-resistant, commonly used for packaging textiles, cosmetics, and stationery due to their clarity and effective product display capabilities.
