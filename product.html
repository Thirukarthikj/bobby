LD Bags

LD bags, or Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE) bags, are a type of plastic bag made from low-density polyethylene. They are widely used due to their flexibility, strength, and resistance to moisture. LD bags are known for their versatility and are commonly used in various industries for packaging and storage purposes.

LD bags are available in an extensive array of sizes and thicknesses, allowing them to be tailored for specific needs, from small, lightweight items to larger, bulkier goods.

HM Bags

HM bags, or High Molecular High-Density Polyethylene (HM-HDPE) bags, are a type of plastic bag made from high molecular weight high-density polyethylene. These bags are known for their strength, durability, and resistance to punctures and tears.

They are available in various sizes, thicknesses, and designs to meet different packaging needs.

LD Roll

An LD roll, or Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE) roll, is a continuous sheet of low-density polyethylene material wound onto a roll. This form of packaging material is versatile and commonly used in various industries due to its flexibility, strength, and protective properties.

LD rolls can be used in a wide range of applications, making them a versatile choice for packaging and protective needs.

PP Bags

PP bags, or polypropylene bags, are a type of packaging made from polypropylene resin, known for its versatility and durability. These bags are widely used across various industries due to their strength, resistance to moisture, and ability to be recycled, making them an environmentally friendly choice.

PP bags are versatile and can be used in a wide range of applications across different industries.

PET Pouches

PET pouches, or polyethylene terephthalate pouches, are packaging containers made from PET plastic, known for their clarity, strength, and versatility. These pouches are widely used across industries due to their ability to preserve product freshness, enhance shelf appeal, and provide reliable packaging solutions.

They are available in various sizes, shapes, and forms (stand-up pouches, flat pouches, etc.), making them suitable for a wide range of products.

BOPP Bags

BOPP bags, or Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene bags, are a type of packaging made from polypropylene film that has been stretched in both the machine direction (MD) and across the transverse direction (TD). These bags are highly versatile and are used across various industries due to their excellent clarity, strength, and moisture resistance.

BOPP bags are available in various types including flat bags, gusseted bags, and resealable bags, catering to different packaging needs.

PVC Pouches

PVC pouches, or Polyvinyl Chloride pouches, are versatile packaging solutions made from PVC material, known for its durability, clarity, and flexibility. These pouches are widely used across industries due to their excellent protective properties and ability to enhance product visibility.

They are available in various forms including zip-lock pouches, stand-up pouches, and pouches with hanging holes, catering to different packaging needs.
